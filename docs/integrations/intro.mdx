## How integrations work

Gladys Assistant is an **open-source project**, developed by a **community of home automation enthusiasts**, with one goal: a smart home that is **simple, local and respectful of your privacy**. Connecting a device should not depend on a closed cloud or on a single manufacturer. That is why integrations come in two flavors, side by side in the catalog above and in the **Integrations** screen of Gladys:

- **Native integrations** ship with Gladys. They cover the **open protocols** we recommend for any new installation: [Zigbee2MQTT](/docs/integrations/zigbee2mqtt/) for thousands of Zigbee devices without any vendor hub, [Matter](/docs/integrations/matter/), the industry standard, 100% local, and [MQTT](/docs/integrations/mqtt/), the universal glue of DIY projects.
- **Community integrations** cover everything else. Each one is a small program packaged as a **Docker container** and published on GitHub by a Gladys user. Gladys runs it in a **sandbox isolated from its core** and talks to it through a dedicated API, so anyone can publish one, in any language, without review, while your instance stays safe.

## Install an integration

In Gladys, open **Integrations**: native and community integrations are listed together, by category. For a community integration, click **Install**: Gladys pulls the Docker image, starts the container and generates its interface (devices, discovery and configuration). You can also install one directly from its GitHub repository URL.

The catalog inside Gladys refreshes every hour, so a newly published integration shows up at most one hour after its release.

## Your device is not covered?

- Ask on the [forum](https://community.gladysassistant.com/): another user may already be working on it.
- **Build it yourself**: community integrations are the simplest way to create and publish an integration, with no pull request and no validation. Follow the [developer guide](/docs/dev/external-integrations/).
- For custom or experimental setups, [Node-RED](/docs/integrations/node-red/) and [MQTT](/docs/integrations/mqtt/) let you wire almost anything to Gladys.

The full source code of Gladys is available on [GitHub](https://github.com/GladysAssistant/Gladys).
